About HITOP

CEO Message

A Constant Challenge
for a Better Future

Built on long experience and specialist engineering, HITOP Co., Ltd. is an infrastructure company that manufactures and supplies bridge expansion joints, non-slip nosings and wood decking. Drawing on 25 years of field experience and technical know-how, we have developed high-quality products designed for safety and durability.

Our patented technologies and proven delivery record have earned recognition for our technical competitiveness, and rigorous quality control and customer-focused service continue to make us a trusted partner.

Committed to making the best products, HITOP never stops researching and improving, and we will keep striving for excellence in quality and technology.

We sincerely appreciate your continued support. Thank you.

CEO, HITOP Co., Ltd.

Yong-hwa Lee
