Milestones

Our Journey Since Founding

Through patent registrations, G2B listings and expanded production facilities, we have built a supply system covering bridge expansion joints, non-slip nosings and wood decking.

  1. 2020

    The Beginning of HITOP

    • HITOP Co., Ltd. founded

  2. 2021

    Entering Public Procurement

    • Bridge expansion joints listed on the G2B (KONEPS) shopping mall

  3. 2022

    Securing Proprietary Technology

    • Patent registered: Expansion joint with improved waterproofing (No. 10-2375750)
    • Patent registered: Method of connecting expansion joints at bridge curbs (No. 10-2401961)
    • Non-slip nosings listed on the G2B shopping mall

  4. 2023

    Strengthening Technical Competitiveness

    • Patent registered: Reinforced widened pedestrian bridge (No. 10-2503300)
    • Certified as a Venture Business

  5. 2024

    Expanding the Production Base

    • Patent registered: Vibration- and noise-reducing expansion joint (No. 10-2622949)
    • Patent registered: Expansion joint with adjustable anchor angle (No. 10-2671452)
    • Relocated to a new, larger factory

  6. 2025

    Expanding the Product Portfolio

    • Wood decking listed on the G2B shopping mall

  7. 2026

    A Leap Toward Global Procurement

    • Designated as a G-PASS company
