Installation Guide

What to Check Before Installation

Consider wood movement, drainage and the substructure together to maintain consistent quality.

01

Standard Dimensions

Thickness 30–50 mm and width 90–120 mm as standard. Custom sizes are available to suit site conditions.

02

Fastening Method

Stainless steel screws or hidden clips, selected according to wood species and substructure.

03

Maintenance

We recommend applying oil stain or water repellent once or twice a year. Oil treatment improves the durability of larch.
